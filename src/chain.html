The provenance chain

Eight links from person to buyer. Each can prove one narrow thing.

  • Providers state each link
  • irlpls shows it
  • irlpls checks none of it
  1. 1

    Real person

    Enrolment

    The person is identified before any work.

    Requirement

    ID + liveness check by a named, audited vendor; re-checked at least monthly

    • Proves Someone passed an identity check
    • Can't prove That they did this capture

    Card fieldPerson

  2. 2

    Captured locally

    Capture

    A product on the C2PA Conforming Products List records the scene; where, only if the position is authenticated.

    Requirement

    A product on the C2PA Conforming Products List; place counts only if the position is authenticated (GNSS alone is easy to spoof)

    • Proves A listed product made the capture (the seal binds the bytes); where, only if the position is authenticated
    • Can't prove That the scene was real

    Card fieldsCameraPlace

  3. 3

    Genuine device

    Device attestation

    A server checks the app and phone are genuine.

    Requirement

    Hardware key attestation (Android Key Attestation, Apple App Attest), not revoked. Play Integrity checks the app and device, not a key

    • Proves Genuine app passed a check
    • Can't prove It stayed uncompromised afterwards

    Card fieldApp

  4. 4

    Sealed capture

    Signing

    A key seals the file and what it says.

    Requirement

    C2PA manifest with hard binding; the signer's certificate chains to the C2PA Trust List

    • Proves Who signed; unchanged since
    • Can't prove That its statements are true

    Card fieldsCameraPlaceContent

  5. 5

    Known timestamp

    Time-stamping

    A third party records that it existed.

    Requirement

    RFC 3161 token from an independent TSA, or a transparency-log entry

    • Proves Existed no later than T
    • Can't prove That it was captured at T

    Card fieldTime

  6. 6

    Tracked in transit

    Chain of custody

    Every transfer and store is logged: custody, not integrity.

    Requirement

    Hash, holder and time for each hop, in an append-only transparency log

    • Proves Custody: who held it, and when
    • Can't prove That it is unchanged (the seal shows that), or anything about an unlogged hop

    Card fieldOptional card field

  7. 7

    Independently verified

    Appraisal

    Someone other than the signer checks it all.

    Requirement

    Validation by a party other than the signer, against a published policy; cross-checks need 2+ independent sources

    • Proves A named party checked stated rules
    • Can't prove More than those rules cover

    Card fieldCross-check

  8. 8

    Delivered to you

    Delivery

    The buyer gets the result, checks shown.

    Requirement

    Signed receipt binding file hashes, your request and your nonce

    • Proves What was checked, and what wasn't
    • Can't prove That your use is sound

    Card fieldOptional card field

Where the levels come from. Each link is read from the card fields shown under it, its guarantees. A link counts only what a card actually states: Sealed capture needs a stated signing mechanism (C2PA, a manifest), and Known timestamp needs an attested time (RFC 3161, a log). Levels: Evidenced, Claimed, Reported (a time stated, not attested) or Not stated.

Terms

Liveness check
Shows a live person, not a photo, is present.
Conforming Products List
C2PA's list of cameras and apps that passed its conformance programme.
Key attestation
The phone's secure hardware vouches that a signing key lives in it. Play Integrity is not this: it checks the app and device.
C2PA manifest
Signed record of who made a file and how, attached to it.
Hard binding
A fingerprint of the exact bytes; any change breaks it.
RFC 3161 / TSA
A time-stamp authority signs a fingerprint with the time.
Transparency log
A public record that can only be added to.
Nonce
A one-time code you issue when ordering. A receipt carrying it was issued after your order; it says nothing about when the capture was made.
