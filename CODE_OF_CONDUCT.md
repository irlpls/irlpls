# Code of conduct

Be respectful. Assume good faith. Disagree about ideas, not people.

Not acceptable: harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, doxxing, or
publishing someone's private information.

When describing providers, stick to checkable facts and cite sources.

Maintainers may edit, reject or remove contributions and block people who break
these rules. To report a problem, use the contact in SECURITY.md (hello@irlpls.com).
